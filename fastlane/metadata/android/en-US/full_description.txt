LangSwap translates single words between French, English and Spanish, with German, Italian and Portuguese as secondary languages. Everything works offline: the dictionary is downloaded once, one language at a time, and searches never leave your phone.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Type a word in any language, or pick the source language; translations are grouped by meaning, with gender, part of speech and region.</li>
<li>Inflected forms are recognised: plurals, conjugated verbs, feminine forms lead to their base word.</li>
<li>When no direct translation exists, LangSwap goes through another language (for example Spanish → English → Italian) and says so.</li>
<li>Tap a translated word to copy it, or to search it in turn — no keyboard needed.</li>
<li>Search history on the device, deletable entry by entry.</li>
<li>Interface in French, English and Spanish; follows the system light/dark theme.</li>
</ul>

<b>Dictionary</b>
Built from Wiktionary (French, English, Spanish, German, Italian and Portuguese editions, extracted by wiktextract) and downloaded from the app's settings: about 66 MB for French, English and Spanish together.
